# ACPM Controlled Pilot — Daily Issue Log

## Instructions
- Log every issue, question, or observation during the pilot.
- Use one row per issue.
- Update the status as issues are resolved.
- Keep a parallel Excel backup.

## Severity Levels
| Level | Label | Response |
|-------|-------|----------|
| 1 | **Critical** | Stop work. Log issue. Contact development team immediately. |
| 2 | **High** | Log issue. Workaround may exist. Review within 24 hours. |
| 3 | **Medium** | Log issue. Review within 1 week. |
| 4 | **Low** | Log issue. Nice-to-have improvement. |
